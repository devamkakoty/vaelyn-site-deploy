The System About

Your collection. One intelligent system.

ZALLIA is an integrated system engineered for the care, preservation and enjoyment of your finest horology, couture and curated collections.

Discreet sensing keeps it connected as your pieces move across residences, yachts and journeys. Its intelligence brings your collection together with your plans, curating for any destination or occasion and coordinating every preparation.

A short film without dialogue. At the Dubai Residence, a tourbillon watch is removed from Island Drawer No. 1 in the master bedroom, and Zallia updates its record. Later, aboard M/Y Aurora, the same watch is detected in the master cabin, with its provenance, service and care on record, and a suede handbag is removed from Display Cabinet No. 2. Each time, Zallia updates without being asked. It closes on the line: The record that keeps itself. Private intelligence across every residence and yacht.

01The record

Each piece,
known in full.

Kept current as each piece moves, without asking anything of you.

The watch from the film is one example. High jewellery, rare leather goods and collectible footwear are held the same way.

02The rooms

The rooms keep the record.

Fine readers set into the thresholds of each dressing room, wardrobe and store recognise when a piece passes, so one record keeps pace across every property without anyone updating it. Discreet sensors watch over the conditions each piece is kept in. When a piece leaves for a specialist, the record says so. It never guesses.

03The intelligence

It knows what is ready.

Ask about the day, the journey or the season ahead. ZALLIA answers from the record the rooms keep current, knowing what is where and what is ready in every home, and offers ideas from your own collection you may not have considered. The choice is always yours.

One collection, every home. Only what is ready is ever proposed.
