Who we are

ZALLIA is developed by Vaelyn Limited, a company registered in England & Wales. Vaelyn Limited is responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at kimberley.g@vaelyn.co.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, your email address and anything you write when you request a private preview or email us. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

To reply to you personally, to arrange a private preview or conversation, and to keep a record of that correspondence. We do not sell your details, and we never use them for unrelated marketing.

Our lawful basis

We use your details because you asked us to contact you, and because replying to an enquiry is in our legitimate interest and yours.

Who else handles it

Requests sent through this website pass through a form delivery service that forwards them to our inbox, and the website itself is hosted by GitHub, which keeps short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses to run the service securely. These providers act on our behalf and may process data outside the United Kingdom under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

For as long as our conversation continues, and no longer than two years after our last contact, unless you become a client, when separate terms apply.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to how we use it. Write to kimberley.g@vaelyn.co and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response, you may contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
