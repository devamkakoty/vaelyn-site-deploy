The System About

One system.
Four layers.

A record for every piece, rooms that keep it current on their own, intelligence that draws on it, and a team that acts on it. Each layer feeds the next, so the whole system stays true in every residence and aboard every yacht, without anyone having to keep it up to date.

01The record

Every piece,
and its whole story.

Each piece carries its provenance, papers, service history, condition and value. It also carries its life: who in your household last cared for it, where it went and when it returned, whether to the pressing room, the packing room or a watchmaker in Geneva. Where something is not known, ZALLIA says so, rather than guess.

02The rooms

Your homes,
quietly aware.

Together, we plan each installation room by room: the dressing rooms, wardrobes, stores and cabins that matter to you. There, ZALLIA is built invisibly into the joinery, and the rooms themselves keep the record current as pieces come and go, between homes and aboard. Conditions are watched with the same care; your team is called only when something needs them.

03The intelligence

Ideas worth
considering.

Ask for the evening, the week or the season, and ZALLIA offers considered ideas from your own collection: pairings of colour, material and proportion you may not have thought of, drawn only from pieces that are ready. It comes to know your taste over time. The choice is always yours.

04Your team

Ask once.
It is handled.

One request becomes a clear brief for everyone who cares for your collection, in every home and aboard. Your team carries on as they always have; ZALLIA asks them only when something needs a person’s judgement, and you hear back when everything is ready.

Your collection. Your discretion.

ZALLIA gives you complete control of who can see what.

Your collection remains yours. Decide which people can view specific categories, pieces or information, while the rest stays private.

  2. 02

  3. 03
